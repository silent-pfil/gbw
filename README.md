# GBW

*Somewhere, a world goes quiet. Not from disaster — from arrival.*

A sci-fi trilogy about belief, biology, and what happens when the cosmos stops being a separate question from either one. Free to read, built to be read immersively, and still being written.

## Read it

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser, or enable GitHub Pages on this repo and visit the published link. From there, all three books are live and fully interactive.

Each book is built as an immersive reading experience, not just a text dump:

- **Ambient sound** — a fully procedural soundscape (Web Audio API, no external audio files) that shifts tone and texture as the story's mood changes, chapter to chapter.
- **Color that moves with the story** — the whole page's palette drifts between eight emotional registers as you scroll, from quiet dread to full cosmic wonder.
- **Hands-free auto-scroll** — paced for a comfortable, unhurried read, so you can sit back and let it run if you'd rather listen than scroll.

Books II and III also carry two small pinned orbs — one tracking a quiet, recurring presence, one tracking something rarer — that brighten at points the story earns rather than on a fixed schedule.

Both the ambient sound and auto-scroll are toggled from the two pinned buttons in the top-right corner. Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion` and works down to mobile.

## A note on how this was made

This trilogy was written collaboratively with Claude (Anthropic), across many long sessions of outlining, drafting, and revision. Every idea, character, and story decision is human — the process was closer to having a very patient, very well-read co-writer than to automated generation. If that matters to how you engage with the work, now you know.
